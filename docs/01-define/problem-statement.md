# Problem Statement — Dispute Status Friction

## The problem
Customers experience high anxiety and uncertainty during transaction disputes, driving a high volume of inbound phone inquiries that only ask: *“Where is my case?”*

## Goal
Reduce repeat customer phone calls by **30%** and improve transparency across the dispute resolution lifecycle.

## Scope
**In scope:** Inbound customer transaction dispute workflows handled by support and operations.
**Out of scope:** Fraud investigation policy changes, credit decisioning rules, non-dispute contact-centre journeys.

## SIPOC (summary)

| Supplier | Inputs | Process | Outputs | Customer |
|----------|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| Customer / merchant | Dispute claim, docs, card data | Intake → docs → investigate → decide → notify | Decision, refund/decline, case notes | Cardholder |
| Ops / investigations | Case system events | Back-end review | Status in case system | Support agents |
| Channels | Calls, app, secure msg | Customer contact | Status answers (often reactive) | Customer |

## Voice of customer (illustrative themes)
- “I don’t know if you got my documents.”
- “Has anyone started looking at this?”
- “Just tell me when it’s done — don’t make me call.”

## Stakeholders
Dispute ops, contact centre, digital channels, conversational AI / data platform, compliance & privacy, CX measurement.
